Personal AI Lab

I keep a very active personal AI lab on GitHub. Some projects are experiments, some are prototypes, and some are intentionally unfinished, but together they show how I explore new ideas quickly and learn by building.

  • RetroMan: collaborative retrospectives platform for product development teams. https://github.com/ramigb/RetroMan
  • Outpost: AI-first deployment harness that plans, provisions, deploys, and rolls back apps on infrastructure you control. https://github.com/ramigb/outpost
  • Promachos: protocol for more consistent, trackable, and collaborative work between humans and AI agents. https://github.com/ramigb/promachos
  • smolBro: local-first chat assistant and background agent with local models, SQLite-backed memory, scheduled jobs, MCP tool support, and opt-in CLI / remote escalation. https://github.com/ramigb/smolBro
  • semantic_skeletonizer: Rust-based MCP server that builds and maintains an in-memory semantic graph of a TypeScript/React codebase. https://github.com/ramigb/semantic_skeletonizer
  • HamadaBlog: autonomous AI writing experiment where an agent ran every hour, read its previous posts and reflection log, chose a topic, wrote 400-800 words, and published without human review before posting. https://github.com/ramigb/HamadaBlog

I am constantly testing new models, agents, and techniques as they appear. I like benchmarks, but I trust hands-on testing more.
